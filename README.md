## 🚀 Featured Work

### 🏙️ Jakarta Pedestrian Detection Pipeline — LSE Research
> End-to-end CV system for urban economics research

- Processed **1,003,847 Google Street View images** across 261 Jakarta neighborhoods
- Two-stage pipeline: **YOLOv11** detection + **EfficientNet-B4** classification (4-class gender/age taxonomy)
- Sequential transfer learning: PA-100K → PETA → Jakarta-specific fine-tuning
- **8,400+ annotations** in CVAT with κ=0.87 inter-annotator agreement
- **7-configuration ablation study** proving necessity of local domain adaptation
- Validated against **48 field observation sites** (r=0.73 correlation)
- Designed **Spatial Uncertainty Index** for transparent error reporting
- Co-authored research paper on spatial independence of female pedestrian presence and street quality
